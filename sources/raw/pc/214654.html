Am I Enough

Dev Parth

Am I just a shadow, lost in the light,
Fading away in the silence of night?
A whisper too quiet, a dream left behind,
A fleeting thought in someone’s mind?

I reach for more, but slip, I fall,
Maybe I’m nothing—maybe that’s all.
Every step feels heavy, every path unclear,
Am I too weak to silence this fear?

They say the strong bend, but never break,
But what if I can’t bear the weight?
What if my voice is drowned in the tide,
And all I have is the doubt inside?

Yet deep within, a fire remains,
A spark untouched by loss or pain.
Maybe my cracks let in the light,
Maybe I still have strength to fight.

So I will rise, though my hands may shake,
With every wound, a step I take.
And even if I stand alone,
I am enough—I am my own.

Dev Parth

 · 2006 · Gorakhpur

My name is Dev Parth. I am a poet who writes not to impress, but to understand. For me, poetry is not a performance. It is a quiet conversation with myself, the kind that usually happens after midnight, when the world is silent and I no longer have to pretend to be strong. I write about being human. About the strange weight of memories. About growing up without losing the softness that once defined us. My words often come from the parts of me that do not speak in conversations , the parts that wait patiently until I am alone. I am drawn to the tension between strength and vulnerability. I believe we all carry entire universes inside us, yet we often reduce them to a simple “I’m fine.” My poetry tries to expand that silence. It gives space to emotions that refuse to stay quiet. Alongside writing, I study Computer Science. Logic shapes how I think, but poetry shapes how I feel. One teaches me structure; the other teaches me honesty. I exist somewhere between both. If my poetry feels real, it’s because it is. There is no character. No mask. Just me trying to make sense of my own echo. I am Dev Parth. And I write for the ones who are learning how to exist without losing what makes them soft.
